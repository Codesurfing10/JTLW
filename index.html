❓ How to Use JTLW

JTLW is a voice-activated AI agent powered by Google Gemini 1.5 Flash.

🚀 Getting Started

  1. Click ⚙️ Settings and add your Gemini API key (free at aistudio.google.com)
  2. Click the 🎤 microphone button or press Space
  3. Speak your command — results appear instantly

💬 Example Commands

  • "JTLW, top 5 stock picks from today's scanner"
  • "What are the sailing conditions in San Diego?"
  • "Build me an F1 race car with interactive 3D components"
  • "Create a 3D solar system model"
  • "What are current weather and tide conditions?"

🔬 Innovations Gallery

Every response is automatically saved to the gallery below the agent panel. 3D creative results are interactive — click View 3D to open them full-screen.

🌊 Data Sources

  • Weather & Tides: NOAA APIs (no key required)
  • Stocks: Alpha Vantage (optional key) + Gemini AI analysis
  • 3D Graphics: Three.js generated by Gemini
